## Lead qualification vs. lead scoring: get this straight first

The two terms get used interchangeably, and mixing them up leads to buying the wrong thing.

Lead scoring ranks your leads against each other. It produces a number, and it works from data you already have: firmographics, email opens, page visits, form answers. Scoring tells you who to call first.

Lead qualification decides whether a lead should be in the queue at all. It's closer to pass/fail against your criteria. A lead can score 92 and still never buy because nobody checked whether they had budget or authority.

Scoring sorts the pile. Qualification decides what belongs in it. Most tools do one properly and the other as a checkbox feature.

Sit with that fourth row for a second. A prospect who messages at 9pm asking whether you cover their postcode, whether you can do Thursday, and what the deposit is — that person is qualifying themselves in real time. No enrichment database can read that conversation, and no CRM scoring model can respond to it.

## A five-question check before you buy anything

1. **Where do your leads actually talk to you?** Write down the real answer, not the aspirational one. Forms, chat, SMS, and DMs point to different products.

5. **Who maintains it?** An agent needs someone to build, test, and supervise it. Tools that ship templates and a community (CloseBot includes 15+ templates on paid plans and 50+ on annual, plus a community layer) lower that bar, but they don't remove it.

## FAQ

**Is lead qualification software the same as lead scoring software?**
No. Scoring ranks leads against each other — who to call first. Qualification decides whether a lead belongs in the queue at all, testing it against criteria like budget, authority, need, and timeline. Many tools do both, and usually one of them properly.

**Does CloseBot work with Instagram and WhatsApp?**
Only through your CRM. Its native integrations are HighLevel, HubSpot, LeadConnector, and custom CRMs, and it answers the text channels connected there. There's no standalone Instagram or WhatsApp connection of its own.

**Can AI tools actually qualify leads accurately?**
They qualify well when the evidence comes from what a buyer said, and poorly when it's inferred from thin behavioural proxies. A model guessing intent from page visits generates weak signals. A system reading qualification from the prospect's own questions has something a rep can check.

**Do I need a dedicated tool if I already have CRM scoring?**
Not necessarily. Native CRM scoring and workflows cover modest volumes well. Dedicated tooling earns its place when qualification has to happen somewhere the CRM can't see — inside a live conversation, at 10pm, before any record exists.
